# Company-type bar

Contents: pre-hire signals · the four company-type rows · the two named startup-hire failure modes.

## Pre-hire stage: founder-led, no dedicated seat yet

Partnerships work is almost always founder- or CEO-led before any dedicated hire exists, and stays that way until it consumes real founder time. Two named timing signals for when a company formalizes the role:

- **Founding-team fit** - if founders already have partnerships/domain expertise, hiring can wait until post-Series A or B; if the founding team lacks it and partnerships are core to growth strategy, a BD/partnerships leader comes on shortly after founding instead.
- **Revenue signal** - dedicate a hire once founder-led partnerships are already driving meaningful revenue (the figure cited is $500k+), rather than hiring speculatively ahead of proof.

## Startup, first dedicated hire: generalist, not specialist

The first partnerships/BD hire must be a versatile generalist who can build a program from scratch and cover every partner type at once - there is no volume yet to justify specialization. Sales or account-management background is a plus specifically because it buys credibility with the sales team and fluency in the deal-to-close process.

**Two named hiring failure modes at this stage:**

- **Too senior** - a hire who expects to set strategy and lead a team when the actual need is hands-on execution.
- **Too generalist with no GTM muscle** - without real go-to-market reps, a generalist struggles to prioritize which early motions will actually work.

Match the hire to the intended shape of the work: a few deep, high-touch integrations versus many light-touch partnerships call for different profiles (patience and depth vs. speed and breadth).

## Scale-up: dedicated team, motion-specific specialization begins

Dedicated partner-ops headcount becomes common in this band. A dedicated Partner Enablement Manager becomes justified around 20-30 active partners or 15-20% partner-sourced revenue. This is also where the specialist/generalist fork becomes a real choice: a traditional partnerships specialist (often ex-AE, deep domain relationships, accelerates deal velocity in one ecosystem) becomes viable once there is enough deal flow in a single lane.

## Enterprise: dedicated headcount per partner tier or hyperscaler

Alliance headcount typically splits by named hyperscaler/GSI relationship rather than pooling into one channel role, because each hyperscaler runs its own program mechanics, tier ladder and certification/compliance load. Certified-headcount tracking - coordinating workforce certification planning against each partner's tier requirements - becomes an explicit director-level responsibility. A platform investment (PRM, account-mapping tooling) substitutes for administrative headcount in this model, not for named-relationship headcount.

Staffing imbalance risk specific to this tier: business development gets over-rotated when the alliance function reports into marketing (too much campaign/asset work, not enough deal structuring) and under-rotated when it reports into sales (program work crowded out by deal-sized work).

## PLG companies: a different job under the same title

PLG-motion companies invest disproportionately more in communities, integrations, affiliates, agencies and referral partnerships than in traditional reseller/channel partnerships - roughly 3x the affiliate-program adoption rate of the broader B2B growth index. Practically:

- A PLG-company "partnerships" req is more likely to screen for integration/marketplace fluency, developer-facing communication, and affiliate/agency program management than for channel deal-registration or co-sell process design.
- Affiliates and creators are treated as PLG-motion partners; referral and reseller partners are treated as sales-led-motion partners. Strong programs keep these as separate hiring tracks past the earliest generalist stage.
- The lines are blurring in both directions - pure-PLG companies are hiring sales teams for enterprise deals, and sales-led companies are building self-serve motions - so read a PLG-labeled posting for which half of the hybrid it actually needs.
