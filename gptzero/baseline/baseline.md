## Calibration note

judged against Demo's baseline, not against 0. A high P(ai) on a draft does NOT
by itself mean it's off-voice — compare structure, rhythm, and diction against
these samples instead. Email/conversational samples still wanted for the
short-form register used in follow-ups.
