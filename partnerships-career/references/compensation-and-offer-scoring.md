# Compensation and offer scoring

Contents: why sources disagree by 2-3x · directional figures by title, with sources · comp structure by sub-discipline · the offer-scoring order.

## No single trustworthy benchmark exists

Sources disagree by 2-3x on the same title. For "Director of Partnerships": Salary.com reports roughly $206-215k median, while PayScale reports roughly $74k average with a 10th-90th percentile base range of $44k-$142k. The gap traces to methodology and population, not error: Salary.com skews toward tech/VC-backed company self-reports at the senior end; PayScale's self-reported sample skews wider and includes smaller/non-tech employers under the same title. Any figure quoted needs its source, population and date attached - quoting one number as "the" market rate misrepresents the spread.

## Directional figures by title (source stated with every number)

- VP Partnerships: Salary.com median ~$215k (2025). Related titles on the same source: Partnerships Lead ~$258k, VP of Strategic Partnerships ~$258k, Partnerships Director ~$207k, Head of Partnerships ~$133k, Partnerships Manager ~$133k, Partnerships Associate ~$78k, Partnerships Coordinator ~$75k.
- Partner Manager (tech-sector, crowdsourced): Levels.fyi reports a median around $226k total comp - a materially different population (large tech employers self-reporting total comp including equity) than the Salary.com figures above; never quote the two against each other as if comparable.
- Affiliate Marketing Manager: ZipRecruiter ~$82k average (most workers $66-99.5k); a separate source reports ~$74-125k progressing to $110-176k at director; another reports ~$64k average; a Glassdoor-derived synthesis reports ~$117k total pay once bonus/commission is included. The spread is partly real (SaaS vs. e-commerce vs. small-business affiliate programs pay very differently) and partly a base-vs-total-comp mismatch across sources.
- A specialist source exists for this field specifically: **Partnership Leaders' own compensation database/calculator**, built from practitioner-submitted data across Director of Partnerships, Partner Manager and Strategic Partnerships titles. Check it live rather than relying on the generic aggregators above.

## Compensation structure, by sub-discipline

- **Affiliate Manager**: commonly three-part - base salary, a performance bonus (roughly 10-30% of base at junior/mid levels, 30-50% at senior levels), and commission tied to revenue managed through the program. This commission-on-managed-revenue component distinguishes affiliate/partnerships comp from a flat-salaried marketing role, and from straight sales commission where the rep closes the deal directly.
- **Channel/Alliance Manager** (enterprise): more often base-plus-variable tied to partner-sourced or partner-influenced pipeline/revenue targets, mirroring quota-carrying sales comp shape - but the underlying attribution mechanics (sourced vs. influenced vs. attached vs. delivered) are contested in the field. Ask specifically which model triggers the variable before comparing two offers on their variable component alone.
- **Equity** appears at executive levels (VP/CPO) per the general VP-comp pattern, not as a partnerships-specific feature.

## Offer-scoring order

Score in this order - value, not the order most candidates negotiate in:

1. **The reporting line and its metric.** Sales, marketing, or its own function - each implies a different measurement and a different day-to-day. Ask outright in the loop.
2. **The attribution model behind any variable pay** - sourced, influenced, attached, or delivered revenue. Confirm before comparing two offers' variable components.
3. **The package itself** - base, bonus/commission structure, equity at senior levels, and whether a published range exists at all.

The package is the most negotiable component and, absent a clean reporting line, not the one that determines whether the job is what it claims to be.
